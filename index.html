nRF52 · Engineered from first principles

Inspired by Daylight Machines, this optimized custom design eliminates every power leak of addressable strips and 5V boost converters — achieving 1–3 months of unplugged runtime on a single 18650 cell at a low BOM cost.

Every component sits on a single 240 × 38 mm 2-layer matte-black PCB (zero loose wires!), snapping cleanly into an ultra-slim 36 mm PETG enclosure printed in one piece on a Bambu Lab A1.

01 / PASSIVE RGB MATRIX

48× 1615 SMD RGB + IS31FL3733

Passive SMD RGB LEDs driven by an IS31FL3733 constant-current matrix IC draw 0.000 mA when unlit and only 0.15–1.0 mA per active bar.

02 / MICROAMP BRAIN + BLE

nRF52832 + 32.768 kHz Crystal

Sleeps at ~2 µA (1,000× lower than Pico W) while keeping accurate RTC time. BLE stays off until you hold Button 1 to customize colors or schedules from your phone.

03 / DIRECT 3.7V + AUTO-DIM

Zero 5V Boost + VEML7700 ALS

Runs directly off 1× 18650 (3.7V) with no 5V boost losses. A VEML7700 ambient light sensor auto-dims LEDs in dim rooms and enters ~4 µA shutdown in pitch darkness.

Single-PCB power budget (~1–3 months)

With a single 3400 mAh 18650 cell used directly at 3.7V (no 5V boost converter) and passive LEDs (`0.000 mA` unlit): 16h indoor daytime @ 80 lux (~2.6 mA) + 8h dark sleep (~4 µA) = ~1.7 mA daily average → ~2.8 months per charge (or 4+ months in Calm mode).

Raspberry · Simplest modular DIY build

No custom PCB required! Built entirely from off-the-shelf modules available in one shipment from Botland: a Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W running readable MicroPython, a cut-to-length WS2812B 144 LED/m strip, a 2× 18650 5V UPS, and a 4 zł IRLZ44N MOSFET for night power cutoff.

Optimized for battery efficiency over sub-second accuracy — delivering ~7–10 days unplugged on 2× 18650 cells.

01 / CONTROLLER

Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W (Eco Sleep)

Underclocked to 48 MHz with Wi-Fi OFF and machine.lightsleep() between 15-second updates (~1.8 mA average). Edit colors anytime in Thonny via USB.

02 / RGB DIODES + MOSFET

WS2812B 144 LED/m + Night Cutoff

Cutting the Botland strip to 24 LEDs (167 mm, 1/hr) halves idle quiescent drain, and an IRLZ44N MOSFET on GP1 disconnects LED power during night sleep (23:00–07:00 = 0 mA).

03 / BATTERY + A1 CASE

2× 18650 Pack + Single-Piece PETG

Two 3400–3500 mAh 18650 cells (~25 Wh) inside a 250×56×52 mm PETG shell that prints flat in one piece on a Bambu Lab A1 (256 mm bed) with zero supports.

52 mm modular PETG case (Raspberry)

Exploded view: front diffuser faceplate, 48-cell matte black light baffle, single-piece 250×56×52 mm PETG body, Pico W + 18650 shield, and rear backplate.

Sized to 250 × 56 × 52 mm with integrated standoffs for the Raspberry Pi Pico W and retention rails for the 18650 battery shield — prints flat in one piece on a Bambu Lab A1 (~145 g PETG, ~4h 10m, zero supports).
